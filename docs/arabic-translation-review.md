# Arabic translation revision

English is the starting language and fallback. German and Arabic remain available,
and an explicitly saved selection is restored on the next visit.

More than 400 Arabic entries were revised against their English source, covering:

- Quiz questions, clinical scenarios, answer explanations, and key answer labels.
- Every section of the EEG reading guide, including its examples and checkpoints.
- Spikes, polyspikes, phase reversal, temporal anatomy, dyshormia, hypsarrhythmia,
  normal variants, and the distinction between epileptiform activity and seizures.
- Neuronal physiology, electrical potentials, current sources and sinks, electrode
  artifacts, spectral power, and selected atlas explanations.
- Numeric thresholds, negations, and electrical units, including millivolts versus
  megavolts and the exponent in the common-mode rejection example.
- Figure captions, frequency-band descriptions, and report guidance formerly
  omitted by the translation extractor.
- Arabic event/seizure counts, including singular, dual, and plural forms, plus
  report branches that previously retained English sentences.

Terminology was also standardized across alternate capitalizations and selected
recurring phrases. Technical electrode names, diagnostic identifiers, modifiers
such as `+R` and `+S`, and user-entered clinical text remain intact.

Regression checks cover the English default, persistence of language selections,
Arabic document direction, critical terminology and numeric qualifiers, rendered
captions, localized report branches, catalog coverage, placeholders, and damaged
character encoding.

Clinical claims remain those of the English source. Other long-form passages
retain machine translations; specialist language and clinical validation remain
outstanding for those passages.
